## 👋 About Me

Hi, I'm Jeffrey, an engineer focused on robotics, embodied intelligence, and integrated software-hardware systems.

My expertise has expanded from artificial intelligence and full-stack development to robot control, motor FOC, real-time communication, robot modeling, industrial desktop applications, and embodied AI.

I aim to build a complete robotics technology stack:

```text
Motor & Drive
      ↓
Real-Time Communication
      ↓
Motion & Force Control
      ↓
Simulation & Modeling
      ↓
Industrial HMI
      ↓
Data Collection
      ↓
Embodied Intelligence
```

I work across software, algorithms, robot hardware, joint drives, control systems, and real-robot deployment.

## 🚀 Open-Source Projects

### OpenArm Vita Studio

An industrial robot-control application for monitoring, 3D visualization, and algorithm integration.

- Qt 6 + QML + Qt Quick 3D
- Real-time robot data and 3D motion synchronization
- Modular and extensible architecture
- Integrated control, configuration, diagnostics, and visualization
- Real-time ZeroMQ and shared-memory communication

### Robot Arm Planner Lab

A visual learning project covering robot kinematics, control algorithms, and trajectory planning from beginner to advanced levels.

- Visualized robotics formulas and algorithms
- Interactive kinematics experiments
- Trajectory-planning and control demonstrations
- Interactive 3D robot visualization
- Designed for robotics beginners and developers

### LeRobot ACT

A robot imitation-learning project for data collection and embodied AI model training.

- Multi-camera and robot-state collection
- ACT model training and evaluation
- Multimodal timestamp synchronization
- Robotic dataset management
- Real-robot deployment and validation

### Motor Control & FOC

Experiments in motor control, joint actuation, and real-time communication.

- Field-Oriented Control
- MIT joint-control mode
- Compliant impedance control
- EtherCAT real-time communication
- Motor operating-state monitoring
